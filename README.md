# Online Sales Analysis Dashboard 📈

## Project Overview

The **Online Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an advanced business intelligence solution developed using **Tableau** to analyze and visualize e-commerce sales data. The dashboard provides actionable insights into sales performance, regional trends, customer behavior, and profitability, enabling data-driven strategic and operational decision-making.

---

## Objectives

The key objectives of this project are to:

* Monitor sales performance across product categories
* Compare regional sales trends and pricing sensitivity
* Analyze monthly sales patterns and seasonality
* Identify top customers based on revenue contribution
* Evaluate profitability by payment methods
* Support strategic business decision-making using visual analytics

---

## Dataset

* **Source:** Kaggle – Online Sales Dataset (Popular Marketplace Data)
* **Link:** [https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shreyanshverma27/online-sales-dataset-popular-marketplace-data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shreyanshverma27/online-sales-dataset-popular-marketplace-data)

### Dataset Features

* **Transaction Data:** Transaction ID, date, units sold, revenue
* **Product Information:** Product name, category, price
* **Customer Insights:** Customer transactions and purchase behavior
* **Payment Methods:** Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal
* **Temporal Attributes:** Year, quarter, month

---

## Data Preparation

Data wrangling and preprocessing steps included:

* Cleaning and standardizing raw sales data
* Creating calculated fields for revenue and profitability
* Deriving temporal fields (year, quarter, month)
* Categorizing products into meaningful business groups
* Ensuring data consistency for multi-dimensional analysis

---

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales Performance by Product Category

* Bar chart comparing revenue, units sold, and pricing
* Identifies high-performing and underperforming categories

### 2. Regional Sales Comparison

* Dual-axis visualization of unit price vs. units sold by region
* Highlights regional demand and pricing sensitivity

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend Analysis

* Time-series analysis of revenue and sales volume
* Reveals seasonality and business cycles

### 4. Top Customers by Sales

* Ranking of customers based on transaction value
* Supports customer retention and loyalty strategies

### 5. Profitability Analysis by Payment Method

* Examines revenue contribution by payment type and product
* Helps optimize payment options and customer experience

## Key Insights

* Electronics generate the highest overall revenue
* Regional pricing significantly affects sales volume
* Sales peak during the first quarter of the year
* Credit cards dominate electronics purchases, while PayPal is preferred for home appliances
